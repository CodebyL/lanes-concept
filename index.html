Fundraisers

Raise money for your team, club, church or charity with an afternoon or evening on the lanes, customized to your group.

  • Party room for birthdays, special occasions and meetings
  • Bumpers available on every lane
  • Food from Cafe 56 and drinks from Grunts Sports Bar on site
How a bowling fundraiser works
  1. The lanes set one rate that covers bowling, shoe rental, food and a beverage.
  2. You set the ticket price for your goal, with five or six bowlers to a lane.
  3. Spread the word and pre-register your bowlers. The lanes can help get it out on social media and email.
  4. Subtract the lane rate and your costs. Your organization keeps the rest.
  5. Add 50/50 raffles, auctions or lane sponsorships to raise even more.

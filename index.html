More than bowling

Come for a game. Stay for the rest.

Sports bar

Grunts Sports Bar

The game on 18 HD screens, with darts, a pool table and a jukebox. Full bar, plus burgers, sandwiches and pizza.

Snack bar

Cafe 56

A full-service snack counter with a menu served in "frames": starters, wraps, quesadillas, burgers, pizza, flatbreads, breakfast and sweets.

Pro shop

Epic Edge Pro Shop

Custom-fit balls, shoes, bags and accessories, with lessons and appointments on request. Not open Sundays.

Between frames

Game Room

Something for the kids, and the competitive adults, while you wait your turn.
